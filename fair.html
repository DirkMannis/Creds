How it works

  1. When a board opens, the server picks a secret random 32-byte salt and shuffles the 400 prize tickets with it (192 Double Up, 2 Big Send, 1 Host Tip, 205 Patron). Play #k draws ticket #k, whatever square you pick.
  2. It publishes a commitment right away: SHA-256("grok-tip-board/v1|stake|n|salt_hex|order"), where order is the 400 tickets as letters (D, B, H, P). The order can't change after that without the hash changing.
  3. When the board closes, the salt and order are revealed. Verify below re-runs everything in your browser: it re-hashes the reveal, re-shuffles the tickets from the salt (HMAC-SHA256 stream, unbiased Fisher-Yates), and checks every play's prize against its ticket.

Beta boards include labeled 🤖 practice players so boards close; removed before real money. They draw tickets in play order like everyone else and are marked 🤖 in the exports.
